# VlaDDoS commercial operating model

Prepared 10 November 2026. This is the operating specification implemented by the repository, not evidence of existing partners, licences, agreements or revenue.

## The product

VlaDDoS sells scoped partner sourcing and commercial origination. A concrete country, industry, capability and operating role define the brief. The network adds qualification, permission, commercial ownership and follow-through. Counterparties contract for and deliver their own financial services directly. VlaDDoS does not accept deposits, hold client money, execute payments or trades, or inherit a provider’s authorisation.

All six participant groups can use both I NEED and I OFFER:

| Participant                | A useful need                                                                | A useful offer                                            | Negotiable revenue path                                                                   |
| -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| International merchant     | Category-eligible acceptance, local methods, payouts or market-entry support | Qualified merchant demand and distribution                | Scoped sourcing fee, provider-paid merchant referral or disclosed success fee             |
| PSP / aggregator           | Country-specific local infrastructure or merchant distribution               | Evidenced payment infrastructure for eligible merchants   | Sourcing mandate, milestone fee or eligible provider revenue share                        |
| Local payment partner      | International PSP relationships, technology or distribution                  | Country-specific operating capability and local knowledge | Introduction fee or recurring share, with entity and operating responsibility established |
| Exchange / OTC business    | Lawful corridor, liquidity or settlement counterparties                      | Evidenced business scope, supported assets and corridors  | Fixed commercial sourcing or origination agreement; never a trade executed by VlaDDoS     |
| Affiliate / B2B introducer | Relevant counterparties for a permissioned opportunity                       | Specific business access and originated demand            | Agreed share of VlaDDoS fee revenue, including explicitly allocated sub-partner shares    |
| Strategic partner          | Distribution, technology or a joint market-entry mandate                     | Expertise, technology and commercial distribution         | Scoped project, negotiated milestone fee or recurring commercial share                    |

The website does not quote universal pricing. A signed schedule identifies the payer, scope, deliverables, eligible fee basis, trigger, exclusions, reporting, collection, affiliate shares, duration, termination and dispute process. A brief is free of fee obligation; it is not a promise of availability or an accepted referral claim.

## Regional acquisition thesis

These are hypotheses to validate with real demand, not claims of existing supply or universal category acceptance.

- **Asia:** Start with one merchant or PSP need in a named market, then qualify the required local methods and legal entities. Xendit’s [official product page](https://www.xendit.co/en/products/all-payment-methods) describes country-specific methods. Infrastructure descriptions do not prove eligibility for a particular merchant.
- **LATAM:** Pair a concrete merchant acceptance or payout brief with a provider that can substantiate the required market and business category. dLocal’s [official FAQ](https://www.dlocal.com/faqs/) describes its emerging-market payment infrastructure. BCB’s [Pix participation information](https://bcb.gov.br/en/financialstability/pixparticipants?modalAberto=pix_regulations) distinguishes participation models; a Pix logo alone does not establish the relevant operating role.
- **Africa:** Evaluate mobile-money and local acceptance needs country by country. Paystack’s [official service description](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2130562) identifies locally registered business requirements. Its [international-payment eligibility policy](https://support.paystack.com/en/articles/2127682) excludes categories including cryptocurrency, betting and online gaming; it must not be presented as generic high-risk supply. Yellow Card’s [technical documentation](https://docs.yellowcard.engineering/docs/africa) is a research input, not licence or counterparty verification.

Four source-linked research starting points are available in Acquisition. They are never seeded as partners or qualified offers. The founder deliberately imports a prospect, reviews restrictions, confirms current scope and obtains permission before progressing it. Source review date: 10 November 2026.

## Solo-founder routine

1. **Daily inbound:** Review new briefs in Founder view. Validate the legal entity, website/domain, operating role, business category, country, methods, settlement responsibility and source evidence. Set the owner, expiry and next contact date. Verification is a recorded operator decision; the website does not perform automated KYC.
2. **Demand first:** Select a concrete demand brief. Research a small number of fitting companies through official public sources. Save the fit hypothesis, source and observation date. Company research reads one requested page, not an entire site, and does not scrape social platforms.
3. **Relevant outreach:** Record the applicable contact basis or permission. Prepare and edit a draft. Approve the exact saved text; editing resets approval. Send manually through an appropriate permitted channel, then record actual send evidence. The desk schedules the next manual follow-up seven days later. Stop on refusal or opt-out; suppression blocks future drafting and sending records.
4. **Qualify a response:** A replied prospect with recorded processing permission can become an opportunity in the existing CRM. This does not verify the business or grant sharing permission. Qualification and bilateral sharing permission remain separate gates.
5. **Contract:** For paid sourcing, a verified, qualified client may sign a sourcing mandate before any introduction exists. For success fees or recurring origination, record the actual introduction and commercial agreement. Record all signed obligations and affiliate splits before the first fee statement.
6. **Deliver:** Record real activation for introduced relationships or accepted delivery for a sourcing mandate. Proposed introductions, signed agreements and activation are separate records.
7. **Collect:** Request periodic statements, reconcile the eligible fee, record the invoice and actual collection evidence. Follow up on outstanding recorded fees. Do not calculate VlaDDoS revenue from a counterparty’s TPV, customer balances or unsupported forecasts.
8. **Pay and reconcile:** Check collected balance, activation/delivery, accepted attribution, contractual hold and unpaid amount. Make an authorised payment externally; the desk only records evidence. Export the ledger for accounting. Review gross fees, obligations, reserves, paid expenses and collected net contribution separately.

LinkedIn headline, About copy, four editable post drafts, discovery questions and a practical cadence are in the private Acquisition kit. Nothing is posted automatically. LinkedIn’s [User Agreement](https://ac.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement) restricts unauthorised automated access and messaging; its [community policies](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/professional-community-policies) prohibit spam. Use manual research, permitted tools and relevant messages.

## Attribution and commission rules

- Browser attribution is optional, first-touch and session-scoped. It respects DNT/GPC. A URL code is an unverified claim; it is not sufficient proof of ownership or entitlement.
- A registered code is linked to a legal payee, business contact and signed evidence. One sub-partner level is supported. The sub-partner and its registered parent can be named beneficiaries; multi-level recruiting rewards are not implemented.
- Claims require an opportunity, evidence and contractual window. The operator checks prior relationships, duplicate leads and permissions before acceptance. A second accepted/disputed claim for the same known company and industry is blocked. Exact self-referrals are blocked; entity verification and conservative company merges address identities that cannot be established from an email alone.
- Competing claims must be resolved before company merges. Company names and shared domains are not automatic identity proof. Disputed attribution freezes affiliate releases while preserving accrued obligations and audit history.
- Allocations identify the agreement, accepted claim and beneficiary. Every share is a percentage of **VlaDDoS gross fee revenue**, never the merchant’s processed funds. Parent and sub-partner shares consume the same 100% cap. Allocations are immutable and cannot be added after the first fee statement; changed economics require a new agreement.
- A recurring allocation applies to each statement within its agreement term. It does not grant earnings beyond that term or an undefined lifetime entitlement. The signed termination/tail text governs any continuation, which must be recorded under the appropriate agreement.
- Integer minor units are used. Each statement’s beneficiary share is rounded down with exact integer arithmetic; rounding residual remains with VlaDDoS. Currencies are never summed or converted together.
- Payout eligibility requires collection, activation/delivery, accepted attribution, the elapsed signed payout hold and sufficient unpaid balance. Receipt references make retries idempotent. Partial payouts are supported.
- Paid records remain immutable. An actual affiliate recovery is an additional receipt-backed entry. A collected fee with unrecovered paid commissions cannot be reversed to void. Recovery must be reconciled first; a claim backing signed allocations remains disputed until resolved, rather than silently deleting liabilities.

## Financial definitions

| Metric                    | Calculation                                                                                  |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Gross accrued fees        | All non-void VlaDDoS fee statements, per currency                                            |
| Gross collected fees      | Statements recorded as received with evidence                                                |
| Affiliate obligations     | Statement-specific signed allocations, whether paid or not                                   |
| Payable now               | Unpaid collected obligations with activation/delivery, accepted attribution and elapsed hold |
| Recorded net contribution | Collected fees − all affiliate obligations on those collected fees − recorded paid costs     |
| Retained cash             | Collected fees − actual net affiliate payments − recorded paid costs                         |
| Reserved cash             | Affiliate obligations on collected fees − actual net affiliate payments                      |

Net contribution is before tax and unrecorded overhead. The founder must record all relevant business expenses in the accounting system; this desk is a commercial subledger, not a bank connection, tax return or complete statutory ledger. A payout record never transfers money. Collection and payment evidence is supplied by the authorised operator, not verified through a bank integration.

## Technical boundaries

The existing Node 24 / SQLite service, taxonomy, CRM, notification outbox, growth worker, EN/RU routes, original assets and optional Vercel gateway remain in use. Vercel can now connect directly to the approved managed Turso database using the same SQLite-compatible schema and commercial engine. No operational data is stored in ephemeral function files. New commercial tables reference existing opportunity, introduction, agreement and revenue records. An explicit, transactional SQLite migration relaxes the original introduction-only agreement constraint for sourcing mandates; it preserves agreement IDs, child foreign keys, custom indexes and triggers and checks referential integrity before commit. All other commercial changes are additive.

AI drafting reuses the existing AI Gateway configuration, with at most ten attempted generations per UTC day and no automatic sending. Only public company notes explicitly reviewed by the operator are approved for that request. The live provider and current model ID must be configured and tested before AI generation can be considered operational. Evidence-based template drafting works without AI credentials. AI does not approve partners, contracts, payments or referral disputes.

The local operating path and managed Vercel persistence path are implemented. Turso Starter was provisioned and connected with the owner's explicit approval. A tested notification receiver and executed commercial agreements with actual counterparties remain external operational requirements. No real partner, financial licence, executed agreement or collected business revenue is implied by deployment or synthetic verification.
