# VlaDDoS Network artwork

The active website uses the existing editorial assets in `public/assets/editorial/`: ivory textured paper, black torn fabric, engraved gold and fuchsia thread.

- `exchange.webp`: the engraved V and thread in the opening composition.
- `people.webp`: the physical knot representing a two-sided relationship.
- `paper.webp`: the repeating paper substrate.
- Other retained editorial assets remain available for future compositions; no commercial coverage is implied by artwork.
- `public/icons/thread.svg` and `public/favicon.svg`: the minimal thread seal. The wordmark and V seal in the header are live HTML/CSS.

Text, controls and forms are live accessible HTML. No text is baked into a new screenshot to simulate functionality. The historical character/gambling hero and former PAN identity assets are archived in `research/legacy-assets`, outside the public build. Historical provenance is preserved in `research/LEGACY_BRAND_ASSETS.md` and earlier research notes; those documents do not describe the active UI.

See `research/NETWORK_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` for current typography, materials, interaction and responsive decisions. Existing editorial asset preparation/provenance is recorded in `research/VLADDOS_EDITORIAL_DESIGN.md`. No new generated imagery or claimed licences/clients are introduced by this release.
